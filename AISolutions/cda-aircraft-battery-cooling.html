From a 6 kW heat load to a FreeCAD cooling loop for an electric aircraft battery pack

A 60-second FreeCAD walkthrough of coolant distribution for six battery modules, from the heat-load calculation and tubing layout to component screening.

Conceptual circulation layout · Sustained operation at 6 kW requires a separate heat sink.

In this 60-second walkthrough, I show how I sized and laid out a liquid cooling distribution assembly for six battery modules. The study combines hand calculations, prior cold-plate CFD, catalog screening, and AI-assisted design work in FreeCAD.

Define the heat load and flow path

Each module was sized for 960 W of battery heat and 40 W of DC/DC converter losses. That gives 6 × 1,000 W = 6 kW. The layout uses six parallel branches, with the top and bottom cold plates in series within each module.

Size the coolant flow

I used 60% propylene glycol by volume, consistent with the prior cold-plate study. With a 30°C inlet, a 35°C return limit, and a specific heat of 3.383 kJ/(kg·K), the energy balance gives ṁ = 6 ÷ (3.383 × 5) = 0.355 kg/s minimum. I carried forward the cold-plate study’s nominal flow of 41.4 L/min total, or 6.90 L/min per branch.

Dow engineering guide

Route the tubing and budget pressure loss

I drew the centerlines around the existing pack ports in FreeCAD and sized the main lines, bank headers, and module branches to 25, 18, and 14 mm ID. Darcy–Weisbach, fitting allowances, and the prior plate-pair CFD estimate of 10.93 kPa gave 44.7 kPa along one complete flow path. A 20% allowance brought the pump screening duty to 53.6 kPa at 41.4 L/min.

DOE fluid-flow handbook

Screen the pump and expansion vessel

The Pierburg CWA400 PWM (7.07223.10.0) was a candidate because it offers variable-speed control and its published curve shows headroom at the target flow. That curve uses 50/50 water/glycol at 80°C and 12.5 V, so the PG60 duty still needs verification. For the vessel, 10 L of coolant expanding from 0°C to 60°C gave 0.376 L of expansion. I placed a Reflex S 2 (8707700) envelope at pump suction as a 2 L packaging reference. Its published glycol guidance goes up to 50%.

Add balancing, instrumentation, and service access

The catalog shortlist included IMI TA STAD DN15 valves to trim branch flow, Swagelok SS-65TS16 ball valves for isolation, and a Spirax Sarco Fig 36HP Y-strainer to protect the plate passages. WIKA A-10 transmitters monitor pressure, an ifm SM8000 meters total flow, and two Bosch Motorsport NTC M12 probes track supply and return temperature. The layout also reserves space for Gates Green Stripe hose, fill, bleed, drain, and relief connections.
