01 · Geometric interface

The computed volume fraction defines the refinement target.

Basilisk solves the two-phase flow using geometric volume-of-fluid (VOF) transport. In mixed cells, the interface is reconstructed with piecewise-linear interface calculation (PLIC). Pure-liquid/pure-gas face jumps are also included so that an interface aligned with a cell face remains part of the distance calculation.

At each adaptation pass, the interface segments are copied into a bounding-volume hierarchy (BVH) for distance queries. The segment geometry and the BVH remain fixed during that pass. Refinement therefore follows the current numerical interface rather than a prescribed wave shape or a change in display resolution.

02 · Native quadtree AMR

Four interface bands set the required local level.

The smallest requested width is h = 8 m / 211 = 3.90625 mm. A cell’s proximity is its centre-to-interface distance reduced by half its diagonal, with a lower bound of zero. This whole-cell allowance refines cells whose corners can enter the target band.

The background minimum is level 6. A separate wall rule keeps the fixed wall and a five-h buffer at the finest level. Coarsening uses interface-distance bands expanded by a factor of 1.5, retaining fine cells until the interface has moved farther away. This hysteresis reduces repeated refinement and coarsening near a band boundary.

Adaptation is evaluated every four solver iterations. Because the time step varies, these passes do not occur at a fixed physical-time interval.

03 · Numerical continuity

A mesh change also requires a consistent transported state.

Refinement and coarsening operate on the native solver fields. Liquid area, domain area, and phase sensible energy are checked across adaptation. Conservative phase-weighted thermal transfer and the native momentum refinement callbacks carry the solution to the new grid.

The remapped face flux is repaired with an auxiliary projection before transport continues. Its divergence guard is 2 × 10−10 s−1. The auxiliary potential is a numerical flux correction; physical pressure is obtained through the separate flow pressure solve.

The run uses a VOF Courant limit of 0.4 and a maximum time step of 0.01 s. Local cell size therefore affects both spatial resolution and the admissible transport step.

04 · Reading the film

Mesh-change colours compare successive saved native grids.

Amber: current cells that are refined descendants of a previously coarser native leaf.

Purple: current parent cells whose areas were fully tiled by finer leaves in the preceding saved grid.

The comparison interval is the 0.05 s between saved states. Several adaptation passes may occur within it, so these highlighted cells are not individual adaptation-event counts. The first state has no preceding grid for comparison.

The detail camera changes position while its physical width and height stay fixed. The renderer uses the same native timestamp for the full-tank and detail panels, with no temporal interpolation, repeated states, or synthetic mesh subdivision. The encoded duration is 18.25 s because 365 frames are shown at 20 frames/s; the last physical timestamp is 18.2 s.

05 · Evidence and interpretation

The film presents a checked prefix of an incomplete calculation.

The delivery audit checks the 365-state sequence, synchronized panels, native-time playback, constant physical crop, encoded-file integrity, and full video decode. The numerical-prefix audit includes the per-step history and native-field checks at five representative saved states. These checks support the provenance of the shown output; they do not establish experimental agreement or mesh-converged fragmentation.

Model assumptions

The assumed tank section is 7 m wide and 4.5 m high. A fixed Cartesian full-cell wall representation and impermeable slip conditions are used. Liquid properties approximate LOX; gas properties approximate helium. Initial liquid and gas temperatures are 90 K and 120 K, with a maintained 95 K wall.

A prescribed accelerating-tank frame uses a vertical acceleration of −0.5 m/s² and lateral forcing with amplitude 0.035 m/s² and period 16 s, ramped up over the first 4 s. The initial free-surface slope is 0.14 and the initial velocity is zero.

Sensible-energy transport is solved without thermal feedback on the incompressible flow. Gas equation-of-state effects, phase change, absolute ullage pressurization, and structural response are outside this model. Two-dimensional interface fragments are qualitative computed features rather than validated three-dimensional droplet predictions.

The attempted 100 s run did not complete. The retained 0–18.2 s prefix is separately scoped and numerically/output verified. The pressure-projection failure after that prefix remains part of the study record.
