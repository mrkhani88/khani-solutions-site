Method and assumptions

A generic planar tank, carried continuously through the scenario.

The calculation uses native OpenFOAM 2512 interFoam on 2,118 cells: a planar section with one cell through its 0.1 m thickness and empty front/back boundaries. The inherited section was previously used in a Falcon 9 reference visualization. It is not New Glenn tank CAD, and the dimensional presentation does not make it a three-dimensional CFD calculation.

The fluids are a LOX-like liquid and a constant-density helium-like gas surrogate. The model is incompressible, isothermal and laminar, with surface tension. Prescribed tank-frame acceleration and boundary fluxes represent ground fill, ascent drawdown, cutoff, coast, settling, transfer and an assumed return sequence. The liquid–gas interface is not reset between phases.

A separate receiver tank uses interval-averaged mixture and liquid forcing measured at the donor outlet. This is one-way boundary coupling. Transfer piping, pumps and two-way pressure interaction are not resolved.

The donor tank’s numerical-control history tightened the time controls during the run. Actual measured maximum Courant number was about 0.832, below the retained fatal guard of 0.85 but above the initial target of 0.35. A tighter-control replay is a separate scenario execution, not a bitwise recreation of the archived trajectory.

Results and numerical limits

Settling changed the outlet conditions, but gas carryover remained.

During approximately 600 seconds of coast, the coarse VOF field developed several detached interior gas pockets and wall-wetting liquid. These are non-equilibrium coarse-grid observations, not a prediction of equilibrium bubble shape or a resolved three-dimensional interfacial area.

After approximately 40 seconds of settling, the donor’s transfer-averaged gas carryover was 0.923489% of the mixture volume at its transfer boundary. That observation motivates comparisons of settling duration, outlet placement and acquisition geometry.

The receiver liquid gain and donor liquid loss differ by 0.062117% of the donor-delivered liquid. This measured one-way boundary-coupling difference remains reported alongside the separate tank-wise conservation checks; it is not assumed to be zero.

Numerical inventory, boundedness and guard checks were assessed. Mesh and time-step convergence, experimental comparison and flight validation have not been established. The numerical observations are screening inputs for further work.

What this model can inform next

Pressurant and structural questions need additional physics.

Pressurant collapse factor

A mass-based collapse factor compares actual required pressurant mass with an ideal reference requirement for a matched pressure and expulsion schedule. This run produces no numerical collapse factor.

It supplies ullage-volume history, interface motion and planar interface-length/wall-wetting proxies. A calculation of actual pressurant demand needs coupled energy and compressible-ullage modeling, gas and wall temperatures, heat transfer and any applicable propellant-vapor or autogenous phase-change physics.

Fluid-load cases for structural work

The maximum saved shell-boundary pressure span is 78.645811 kPa at 110 s in the generic ascent phase. It is maximum minus minimum shell-boundary fluid pressure and is invariant to a uniform reference offset. Saved extrema sample approximately every two seconds.

The film’s pressure map is relative to each frame’s minimum on a fixed 0–80 kPa scale. The imposed pressure datum does not establish thermodynamic ullage pressure or actual external pressure.

These relative pressure histories can help frame later load cases. Stress, strength and buckling require a separate structural model with suitable geometry, materials, wall thickness, supports, imperfections and load mapping. None is calculated here.

Independent study and third-party media

Keep the mission context separate from the assumed tank scenario.

The launch clips show NG-2 / ESCAPADE, November 13, 2025. The mission-profile diagram and postlanding recovery photo provide separate NG-3 context from April 2026. They are not footage of this modeled transfer, and the recovery image is not touchdown footage.

New Glenn booster recovery is distinct from upper-stage orbital delivery. The combined whole-tank orbital-transfer and Earth-return sequence in this generic scenario is hypothetical. The official mission-profile diagram shows planned targets rather than validation of achieved orbit or the modeled scenario.
