Model

Streamlines, pressure and lift use inviscid 2D potential flow. Drag is a separate, unvalidated engineering estimate; it does not change the flow field.

The drag polar fits three NASA NACA 0012 SSTm reference points at Re = 6 million. Smooth-wall turbulent friction scales it with speed; thickness and surface length provide approximate shape corrections. Camber enters through lift and surface length. The reference is a different airfoil, so this is a trend demonstration, not shape-specific validation.

Assumptions: 1 m chord, air density 1.225 kg/m³, viscosity 1.81 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s, fully turbulent smooth surfaces. No transition, separation, stall, induced or wave drag model. Low Reynolds numbers and high loading are extrapolations. Arrow lengths use separate capped display scales.

Correlations: flat-plate friction, Fig. 5 · section form factor, Eq. 4-51.
