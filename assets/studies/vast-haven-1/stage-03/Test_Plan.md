# Proposed subsystem TVAC readiness package
Status: test planned; zero physical TVAC tests in this study.
## Article and boundaries
Representative 600W avionics spreader/box on liquid cold plate, coolant feedthroughs and support fixture. Evacuated exterior; do not evacuate a pressurized flight box that requires internal air without a qualified pressure design. This is a proposed test article, not a Haven flight assembly.
Temperature-controlled shroud, coolant supply and supports modeled independently. Laboratory gravity retained; fluid orientation effects must be assessed. Chamber pressure requirement, ramp rates, qualification/acceptance levels and cycle count require program/facility approval; none selected as flight requirements here.
## Instrumentation
Calibrated thermocouples/RTDs at heater footprint center, corners, TIM interfaces, coldplate inlet/outlet, mounting feet, fixture and shroud. Log each electrical power channel, coolant mass flow/pressure/temperatures, chamber pressure and functional telemetry. Record sensor attachment, wire routing and calibrated uncertainty; identify instrumentation heat leaks.
## Sequence
Leak/functional and ambient baseline checks; pumpdown with approved limits; controlled hot/cold balance plateaus at low and nominal dissipation; functional checks at plateaus; cold startup, heater control and loss-of-flow protection checks; tailored thermal cycling and post-test inspection. Numerical illustration shows300W2h,600W3h,off3h with10s output. Actual plateau duration follows stability, not the arbitrary illustration clock.
Proposed balance stabilization gate: all designated temperatures change less than0.1K/h over1h, and measured boundary power is stable. This is a proposal requiring tailoring.
## Correlation and uncertainty
Predict sensor values and time constants before test. Record predicted-minus-measured residuals. Estimate bounded contact conductance/emissivity using selected balance cases with traceable property priors. Freeze fitted parameters and predict a withheld power/boundary condition. Proposed development targets: bulk-sensor residualwithin3K and transient timeconstantwithin15%, reviewed against combined measurement/model uncertainty. No claim these are Vast criteria.
## Deliverables
Pretest prediction report, approved procedure and limits, sensor/channel map, configuration and calibration records, original time histories, anomalies and disposition, correlation parameter ledger, held-out comparison and revised requirement verification matrix. No synthetic measurements are used as validation evidence.
