# Haven-1 CAD evidence and assumptions

Reviewed 2026-10-28. This is an independent reconstruction from public material, not Vast engineering CAD or work performed for Vast. There is no complete released manufacturing model in the sources reviewed. See `evidence-register.json` for exact source URLs and traceable assumptions.

## Public anchors

- Current official envelope: diameter **4.4 m**, length **10.1 m**; published volume is **80 m³ pressurized / 45 m³ habitable**. These measures are different and must remain separate.
- Official hardware counts include **12 solar wings**, **6 control moment gyroscopes**, and **8 wet-trash tanks**. Window diameter **1.1 m**.
- Official hardware images also identify cold plates, a thermal pump tray, batteries, ventilation fans, oxygen trays and MMOD shielding. Existence is documented; complete mounting coordinates and internal dimensions are not.

## Visual reconstruction

The July 2026 official cutaway is the best reviewed internal-layout reference. It puts the narrow axial docking interface and tapered transition at one end, the corridor and crew quarters in the middle, and the common area/lab near the opposite end. The observation dome projects radially from the common-area sidewall; it is not the axial end cap. Exterior artwork shows an opposite-end dark taper.

For the first CAD, X=0 is the docking tip. The 0–2.0 m docking transition, 2.0–8.3 m barrel and 8.3–10.1 m opposite taper are **inferred**, not drawing dimensions. The window’s approximate X=8.2 m position is also inferred. Four visual solar banks with three modeled wings per bank preserve the documented total of twelve; grouping, deployment hinges and panel segmentation remain assumptions.

The exact active radiator surfaces cannot be reliably identified from artwork alone. A named candidate radiator panel is acceptable for later trade studies, but must not be presented as Vast’s actual radiator position/area. A complete 4.4 m-diameter exterior barrel has a different geometric volume from the published pressurized volume; do not force the two to agree silently or claim an inferred inner-shell radius as measured geometry.

## Patent and publication boundaries

- **WO2025072764A1**, assigned in the publication to Vast Space LLC, is a real docking/undocking patent involving spinning spacecraft. It is useful background, **not a Haven-1 hull dimension source**. Do not imply every patent embodiment is flight hardware.
- **ICES-2025-465, ECLSS Trace Contaminant Control Testing**, is a public Vast/NASA technical paper. It supports a later ARS/TCC subsystem and test-method discussion, not a complete vehicle CAD or independent flight-performance validation.
