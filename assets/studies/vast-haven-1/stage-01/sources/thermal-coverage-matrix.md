# Haven-1 thermal and resource coverage matrix

Coverage map for a representative public-source CAD and future integrated 1D + selected 3D study. Not a complete actual flight BOM, released design, validated thermal model or simulation result.

Stage: **CAD review only**. Equipment colors identify systems, not temperatures. Publicly pictured hardware establishes existence, not exact installation dimensions.

## Reference anchors and geometry boundary

- Exterior envelope: published maximum diameter **4.4 m**, overall length **10.1 m**.
- Published **80 m³ pressurized** and **45 m³ habitable** volumes are distinct from the gross exterior CAD envelope. The present CAD is not calibrated to those internal volumes.
- Public counts: four crew, twelve solar wings, six CMGs, eight wet-trash containers and eight MLE lab slots.
- Published **13.2 kW peak solar generation** and **100 W continuous per single MLE** are electrical reference limits, not assumed mission heat generation.
- All unpublished powers, heat loads, flow rates, inventories, temperatures, properties and control settings remain **unassigned (`null`)** in the JSON.

## Required thermal and control interfaces

- **I01 External environment → Hull / shielding / window / solar / radiator:** Solar absorption + Earth albedo + Earth IR + deep-space radiation. Physical boundary required; fluxes, attitude, eclipse and view factors unassigned.
- **I02 Avionics / power / batteries → Mounts, TIMs and cold plates:** Conduction through contacts and structures. Cold-plate function documented; contact layers/properties/layout candidate.
- **I03 Cold plates → Coolant supply/return:** Convection inside pumped channels; pressure loss. Heat path documented; channel geometry/coolant/routing unassigned.
- **I04 Pump and converter losses → Coolant and local structure / air:** Dissipation partition. Candidate energy partition, not assumed 100% directly into coolant.
- **I05 Coolant loop → Radiator:** Internal convection + panel conduction + external radiation. Function documented; active area/UA/interfaces unassigned.
- **I06 Crew / cabin payloads / surfaces → Cabin air and cabin surfaces:** Forced convection + surface radiation + latent loads. Crew/payload presence documented; coefficients, duty and metabolism unassigned.
- **I07 Cabin air → Cabin heat exchanger / coolant:** Forced convection and optional condensation. Representative coupling; HX and moisture model must be defined.
- **I08 Cabin → Dragon IMV boundary:** Forced air mass/species/enthalpy exchange. IMV function/route in tunnel documented; actual flow/boundary unassigned.
- **I09 Interior panels / windows / structure → Each other:** Radiative view factors and conductive attachments. Required full-system network; optical properties and contacts unassigned.
- **I10 Heater patches → Target plate/tank/line/equipment:** Electrical heat through bonded/contact interface. Explicit proposed study elements; flight patch layout unverified.
- **I11 Propulsion and gas-resource nodes → Station walls, mounts and environment:** Conduction/radiation, expansion and duty-driven soakback. Subsystems documented; coupling and loads unassigned.
- **I12 Sensors and controllers → Pumps, bypass/isolation valves and heaters:** Control signal affects thermal transport and power balance. Candidate control architecture; no flight tuning or qualification claimed.

## Scope and accounting rules

- Leave unpublished quantities null until a declared, sourced input or explicitly labeled engineering assumption is selected.
- All equipment electrical input does not necessarily appear as heat in the same thermal node; account for mechanical, RF, stored and exported energy.
- Heater and pump electricity are part of system power and battery resource balances, not independent unlimited inputs.
- Gross exterior envelope, pressurized 80 m3 and habitable 45 m3 are distinct. Current CAD does not establish the internal fluid volume.
- Forced airflow is required for the proposed cabin CFD; do not import Earth-gravity natural-convection assumptions into orbital microgravity.
- Exterior orbital heat rejection is radiative. Conventional ambient-air CFD around the station is outside this study physics.
- OpenModelica system model should exchange reduced-order interfaces with selected 3D submodels rather than claim every component is resolved in one mesh.
- CAD routes and component adjacency are illustrative; thermal/fluid connectivity, leak tightness and mesh suitability remain to be checked.
- No solvers were run for this matrix. No temperature, heater power, coolant flow, energy, optimization or validation result is claimed.
- Do not copy the confidential-marked National Academies deck; use cited openly published company material and public technical papers.
