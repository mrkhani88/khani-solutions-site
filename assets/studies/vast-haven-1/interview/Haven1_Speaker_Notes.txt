## Slide 1

Opening: I built a representative Haven-1 thermal-control study to demonstrate how I approach requirements, active/passive control, detailed thermal and fluid analyses, hardware interfaces and test readiness. The mission context is habitation and research in low Earth orbit. Public dimensions and vehicle references guide the visual reconstruction. Internal hardware and numerical design values are explicitly assumed.
The engineering objective is to maintain avionics bulk 0–55 °C, battery 10–35 °C, payload 10–40 °C and cabin 18–27 °C across the assumed 24h mission. A separate 85 °C spreader-hotspot development limit is an analyst assumption, not a component junction rating. Mission clock starts at assumed sunrise, with 60minsun/30mineclipse. We compare one baseline and an improved configuration and investigate a reduced-flow fault.
The toolchain is OpenModelica for integrated mission dynamics and hydraulic pressure balance, OpenFOAM for liquid-channel CFD, and FreeCAD/CalculiX for geometry, solid conduction and thermoelasticity. These tools demonstrate transferable methods. They do not establish Thermal Desktop-specific proficiency. This project includes a TVAC pretest model and procedure outline, not executed chamber testing.

## Slide 10

TVACmeans thermal vacuum testing. Hardware operates inside a chamber with evacuated exterior and controlled radiative and conductive boundaries. Removing air suppresses exterior convection; pressure alone does not set the hardware temperature. Chamber shrouds, coolant feedthroughs and support fixtures establish the heat paths. Gravity remains. A pressurized article requires appropriate maintained internal pressure and an approved pressure design.
The graph is a computed PRETEST prediction, not test data. Xis test timehours, Yis bulk-box°C. The example applies 300 Wfor 0–2h,600 Wfor 2–5h,thenpoweroffto 8h. Cold boundary uses 223.15 Kshroudand 5 °Csink; warm boundary uses 323.15 Kshroudand 25 °Csink. The representative model uses 30kJ/Kbulkcapacitance,improvedFEMresistance,emissivity 0.8,0.25 m²radiationareaand 0.22 W/Ksupport/wireleak to 20 °C. These are proposed fixture assumptions; chamber fixtures and radiation differ from the flight model.
Thermal balance compares measured steady heat paths and temperatures with predictions. Thermal cycling checks functionality through tailored hot/cold exposures. Place calibrated sensors at heat-source center/corners,TIM interfaces,coldplate inlet/outlet,mounts,fixtureandshroud. Measure electrical power,coolantflow,pressureandfunctionaltelemetry. Predict readings beforetest.
The procedure proposes stabilizationbelow 0.1 K/h for 1h and developmentcorrelationtargetswithin 3 Kand 15%timeconstant, subject to uncertainty and program approval. Fit bounded contact/emissivity against selected data,freezeparameters and predict anothercase. No test levels/cyclecounts are asserted as Vast requirements. The matrix distinguishes numerical work from test readiness and actual testing. The delivered README and evidence map link each job duty to model,plot,requirement,design decision or unresolved test gate. Procurement cost/schedule estimates require actual drawings/quotes; no invented approval is claimed.
