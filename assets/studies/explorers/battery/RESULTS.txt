Computed illustrative battery-module CHT results

FreeCAD exact-solid geometry; Elmer 26.2.1 flow and transient thermal solve.
30 pouch cells represented by one homogeneous anisotropic thermal block.
300 W uniform heat; 1 L/min water at 25 C; initially 25 C; one-hour transient.
Titanium casing, explicit TIM, two opposite aluminum cold plates in series.

At 3600 seconds:
Peak equivalent battery-block temperature: 63.50 C
Volume-mean equivalent battery-block temperature: 51.65 C
Coolant after plate 1, flux-weighted: 26.421 C
Coolant at outlet, flux-weighted: 27.802 C
Instantaneous heat carried out by water: 194.60 W
Instantaneous stored-energy rate: 105.40 W
Accumulated thermal energy stored: 620.97 kJ
Maximum BDF-discrete energy residual over 120 steps: 1.21e-09 W
Smallest numerical temperature over all outputs: 24.97565 C

The low through-plane conductivity (1 W/m/K) limits heat transfer from the
module interior to the cooled faces. The second plate is warmer because its
inlet water has already passed through the first plate. This transient is
still heating at one hour; it does not demonstrate thermal equilibrium.

Scope: an illustrative laminar screening model. Material properties and
contact assumptions are not measurements. Flow is in the transitional Re
range; turbulence, mesh and time-step sensitivity, and experimental
validation have not been completed. Flow-to-thermal transport uses RT0
mass-conservative flux recovery; see MODEL_NOTES.txt and
flux_reconstruction.json for the material 22.45% velocity correction.
The original 84 kPa pressure result is not qualified for hydraulic design.
The video is suitable as a clearly labelled engineering demonstration,
not as proof of a verified battery design or measured performance.
