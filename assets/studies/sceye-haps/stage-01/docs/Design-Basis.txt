PUBLIC-REFERENCE HAPS DESIGN BASIS / STAGE 01
Independent Khani Solutions study, 23 October 2026.

REFERENCE AND GEOMETRY
SoftBank publishes a 65 m Sceye reference class. This revision uses 65 m overall,
including an assumed 64 m axisymmetric envelope and 1 m stern allowance. Maximum
diameter 14.5 m, nose/aft profile, fins, solar patch layout and three-blade display
propeller are estimated/illustrative. It is not a production replica, photograph-
calibrated metrology model or a reconstruction of every newer ST1/SE2 variant.
No hidden internal dimensions were available in the primary sources reviewed.

The older harness patent's side-propeller embodiment is not merged with the
photographed stern-propeller layout. Public patents explain concepts; they do
not prove the current installation. Source IDs are carried on native CAD objects.
All public images retain publisher attribution and a link to the source page.

MODELED MAIN PARTS
Envelope display body, dorsal solar patches, four interpreted tail surfaces,
stern motor/hub/propeller envelope, proposed motor controller, underside mounting
concept, service-pod covers, four illustrative battery modules and BMS, avionics
computer/navigation/telemetry units, power conversion and DC-DC distribution,
telecom RF/antenna envelopes, an optional EO/IR payload, and proposed radiator,
strap, heater and temperature-sensor locations. Proposed equipment stays outside
the helium envelope. Actual count, location and internal construction are unknown.
The cutaway is a zero-thickness display surface; no physical laminate thickness
or mass is inferred. Solid display volumes must not be turned into material mass.
Optics and telecom categories are alternative public mission examples; this is
not a claim that the illustrated arrangement flew together.

CAD QUALIFICATION
Native FreeCAD and STEP checks establish geometric validity and reopening only.
This assembly is not a non-overlapping CHT region model, pressure-qualified
structure, manufacturing drawing or CFD/FEM analysis mesh. The browser triangles
are a display tessellation from the same native objects. No solved temperature
field is painted onto the CAD: colors identify subsystems and evidence/design.

THERMAL ARCHITECTURE / OUR CANDIDATE
Separate insulated battery temperature control from avionics/RF waste-heat
rejection. Evaluate conductive baseplates, finite-contact straps, selected
radiating surfaces, solar shielding and thermostatic heater control. Tail motor
and controller need their own losses/mount/propwash model. No liquid loop or
specific catalog heat pipe is selected. This architecture is proposed, not known
to be Sceye's installed solution.

MODEL PHYSICS TO IMPLEMENT
Conduction through hardware/interfaces/cables; external finite-density convection;
face-specific direct/reflected solar, Earth/sky/hull IR and shading; finite heat
capacity; mode-dependent electrical losses; motor/ESC maps; battery heat, charging
limits, heater energy and state of charge; hull/helium thermal-pressure coupling.
A fixed geographical position does not mean zero air-relative wind. Solar input
must be partitioned between electrical export and local heat without double
counting. The stratosphere is not vacuum. Internal gas pressure/venting must be
specified separately from outside pressure. Reflective/insulating materials need
pressure-appropriate tested properties. MLI vacuum performance is not assumed.

INITIAL NUMERICAL EVIDENCE
Standard-atmosphere and radiative/convective rejection calculations are algebraic
presizing. Effective h, surface properties, view factors, IR fluxes and 200 W
example load are explicit study assumptions. The first screen omits reflected
solar, spreading/contact resistance and time dependence; no radiator is selected.
The three native OpenModelica benchmarks check linear heating/cooling against
analytic solutions and a nonlinear radiative equilibrium. They verify code/tool
behavior only, not this airship's thermal performance or any physical hardware.

LIMITS AND REQUIREMENTS
No OEM heat maps, thermal limits, cell properties, shell allowables, production
thermal architecture or flight telemetry were obtained. Temperature limits remain
TBD by component/mode (operation, survival, startup and battery charging). Use
hot margin = allowed maximum minus predicted maximum, and cold margin = predicted
minimum minus allowed minimum. Keep model and measurement uncertainty separate
from allocated design margin. A solved temperature is not a qualified limit.

STAGED EVIDENCE GATES
1. Research + native CAD + source/requirements registers + initial checks.
2. Repeated diurnal OpenModelica network and independent energy audit.
3. Controlled hardware/optical/interface/heater trades and uncertainty sweeps.
4. Targeted spatial CHT and thermal-stress/optical distortion mapping where needed.
5. Test/model correlation using real measurements and independent holdout cases.
6. Reviewed thermal design and actual hardware integration/readiness evidence.

No hardware test, manufacturer collaboration, standard compliance or operational
readiness is claimed. A proposed test matrix is not an executed test. No fabricated
measurements will be used as experimental validation. Private recruiter emails,
NDA documents, current employer data and interview meeting details are excluded
from the public study and download package.
