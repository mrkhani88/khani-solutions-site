METHOD
1.Freeze Stage03 combined thermal hardware, cap and mission.
2.Reproduce P30 baseline.
3.Compare P300 only.
4.Test Ki0.1,0.5,2 with Kp300; back-calculation tracking60s, same300W cap.
5.Select tested Ki2 on heater-on tracking/headroom criteria, then check air−10K and30s sensor lag.
6.Check capacity100W diagnostic; controller cannot supply unavailable heat.
7.Audit whole-system energy, native controller equations, periodicity and solver refinement.

WHY ENERGY INCREASES
The baseline used less heat while running below its target. Removing that temperature deficit requires more heat. Motor-heater energy, all-heater energy and DC demand are distinct quantities; heater energy is already included in DC demand.
