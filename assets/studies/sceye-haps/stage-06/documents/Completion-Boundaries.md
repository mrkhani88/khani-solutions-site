# Completion boundaries and physical closeout

The Stage 06 deliverable is an independent simulation, documentation and test-planning portfolio. The six guided steps and 13 job-description rows link retained evidence. **No physical test has been performed and no OEM vehicle is qualified by this work.**

## Delivered at the study scope

- Mission/requirements and source/assumption separation; operating/service/energy objectives are chosen study limits.
- Integrated day/night/cold-start thermal-energy analysis, representative electronics detail and design comparisons, with numerical checks.
- Native CAD/model/code and retained outputs, plus explicitly identified representation gaps.
- Instrumented prototype definition, sensor map, test matrix, empty raw-data template, illustrative uncertainty allocation and applicability assessment.
- Synthetic calibration and held-out prediction workflow; generated data remain explicitly labeled.
- Fault simulations, risk/interface records, review checklist and an interview presentation path.

## Evidence still required for physical closure

| Gap | What closes it | Current disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Actual equipment power/limits | Selected part data, verified measured power and approved mission requirements | Unknown; representative numerical assumptions retained |
| Physical article/integration | Manufactured assembly identity, photos, inspection and actual interface repeatability | Planned; no article built |
| Environmental test | Approved facility procedure, calibrated instruments, raw history and actual chamber records | Planned; not executed |
| Model physical correlation | Calibrate measured run; freeze parameters; predict independent measured cases with uncertainty | Synthetic workflow only |
| Structures/optics/RF performance | Allocated gradient/stability/structural requirements and relevant analysis/test | Broad thermal nodes only; detailed thermoelastic/performance evaluation unresolved |
| Qualification/readiness | Applicable controlled standards/categories, accepted verification and accountable signed disposition | Not claimed |
| Prior industry hardware experience | Candidate's substantiated non-confidential professional examples | Outside this independent study; not inferred from portfolio |

A prepared plan is useful engineering work, but it does not change the status of an unexecuted test to physically tested. A successfully solved model is not proof the assumed vehicle can perform the mission. Aborted/failed cases and rejected candidate extrapolations remain part of the evidence rather than being hidden.

No hardware purchase or uncontrolled experiment is necessary to review this portfolio. The physical activities above require a future authorized hardware/test program.
