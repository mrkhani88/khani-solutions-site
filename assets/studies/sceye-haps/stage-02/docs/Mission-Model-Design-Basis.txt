KHANI SOLUTIONS | STAGE 02 | INTEGRATED DIURNAL THERMAL-ENERGY MODEL
Independent public-reference demonstration, 23 October 2026.

1. MISSION AND EVIDENCE
Sceye describes solar/battery-powered platforms operating at 60,000–65,000 ft,
remaining over an area for telecom and observation. Its published April 2026
endurance account includes repeated diurnal station keeping and transit. This
study chooses one illustrative station-keeping day, not a replay of that flight:
20 km geometric altitude, 35 degrees latitude, equinox, local solar time 00–24 h.
The 24 h forcing repeats for eight days; the eighth is displayed after comparing
it with the seventh. A stationed vehicle still encounters relative wind.
Actual missions, payload powers, wind limits, headings and control schedules are
unpublished. Neither the scalar wind-power relation nor the attitude profile is
a flight-dynamics/station-keeping controller or aerodynamic performance model.

2. ONE INTEGRATED NATIVE MODELICA PACKAGE
HAPSDiurnal.IntegratedMission instantiates MissionEnvironment, HullSolar,
Avionics, Battery, Payload, Propulsion, Structure and PowerManager. There are 24
finite-capacity thermal nodes, connected through finite thermal conductances,
reciprocal hull-radiation ports, a common structure, an environmental signal bus
and a conservative electrical dispatch model. Native classes, ports, placements,
connections and source bindings are downloadable. Source-derived website maps
are not OMEdit screenshots. Native OMC introspection checks their components and
connection counts. Modifier bindings also carry sources and sensor feedback.

Avionics: processor, navigation, telemetry, DC-DC converter, MPPT, tray, radiator.
Battery: aggregate core, case/heater interface and external insulation jacket.
Payload: telecom RF core, mounting plate and radiator; no optical payload solved.
Propulsion: winding, housing/bearing proxy, controller and mounting bracket.
Hull/solar: four bare-skin sectors, combined PV/local substrate and bulk helium.
Structure: common service-mount thermal node. The bearing proxy is housing
average temperature; it is not an independently resolved bearing hot spot.

3. ASSUMED GEOMETRY AND THERMAL PROPERTIES
Bare envelope area derives from the Stage 01 display-hull tessellation, about
2019.6 m2; displayed volume about 5621.9 m3 is used only for gas-capacity presizing.
The PV/local substrate effective area is 100 m2, separately assumed. Four hull
sectors each receive one quarter of gross envelope area, with the PV area removed
from the bare crown. Equipment capacities, interfaces, optical properties and
areas are independent assumptions, not as-built material/part data. The two
radiators each use 3.12 m2 effective emitting area, with the opposite face treated
as covered. These lumped areas do not establish a CAD-to-CHT mesh reduction.

Metallic skin: alpha=0.16, epsilon=0.08; PV alpha=0.90, epsilon=0.85. Electronic
radiators alpha=0.15, epsilon=0.85. These are trade inputs, not measured Sceye
coatings. Hull effective areal capacity 180 J/m2/K; PV/substrate 2000 J/m2/K.
Helium C=m*cv, with ideal mass presized at 236.65 K, ambient+250 Pa and cv=3116.1
J/kg/K; fixed-volume thermal approximation. No gas pressure control, ballonet,
variable-volume work, lifting-gas leakage, buoyancy or complete mass closure.
The 60 kWh battery and 200 kJ/K aggregate core capacity are independent assumed
quantities. Do not derive mass from the manufacturer's published energy density.
The source-linked component register gives every thermal-node capacity and start.

4. ENVIRONMENT / ORIENTATION
Nominal standard pressure at geometric 20 km: 5529.3054 Pa. Air T=-56.5+4*cos
[2*pi*(hour-15)/24] degC. The cold sensitivity subtracts 10 K from air only;
it is not a complete cold-weather bound because radiation and wind stay fixed.
Direct-normal solar 1250 W/m2 when Sun is above the horizon, zero at night;
no atmospheric extinction/weather model. Ground albedo=0.2, Earth IR=240 W/m2,
background sky IR=80 W/m2. These are bounding-study inputs, not measured weather.
No diffuse sky solar, terrain, cloud or reflected sunlight harvested electrically.

Equinox Sun vector is computed in local east/north/up coordinates, then rotated
into the body using assumed heading 20+10*sin(2*pi*hour/24) deg, roll amplitude3
deg and pitch amplitude2 deg. The heading sensitivity adds90 deg while holding
prescribed wind/shaft loads fixed; it does not model maneuver or heading-control
power. PV incidence uses three weighted roof facets: 50% crown and25% each side
at45 deg. Body radiator normals, shielding and view-factor assumptions are
explicit in the source. No ray-traced shadows, true CAD view factors or pointing
controller. Solar is gated by actual solar elevation, not body-frame elevation.

Effective convection uses h_ref*sqrt(rho/rho_ref)*sqrt(wind/8). h_ref values are
assumed altitude screening coefficients, not validated correlations or CFD.
Prescribed wind: 8+2*sin[2*pi*(hour-4)/24]+3*trapezoid(hour15–18) m/s, with30 min
ramps. Ground/ascent/descending convection is not part of this station-keeping
case. A stratospheric atmosphere is not modeled as vacuum.

5. HEAT FLOWS AND POWER
Every surface includes signed finite-air convection, direct solar projection,
reflected solar, Earth/sky IR and optional reciprocal hull IR exchange. Radiation
uses kelvin to the fourth power; ambient air is not the radiative sink. Exchanging
surfaces' hull ports receive exactly opposite heat flows. The small effective
hull area obscured by those equipment exchanges is removed from lower-skin
external area. View factors are approximate and must be replaced with geometric
ones. Internal helium couples through assumed0.25 W/m2/K paths; inner-wall
radiation and detailed internal circulation are omitted.

Electrical avionics: processor65–85 W, navigation12 W, telemetry35–50 W.
Payload:350 W night/500 W day plus300 W peak profile11–14 h;35% of electrical
input leaves as RF output,65% is heat. Motor shaft power1200*(wind/8)^3 W;
motor efficiency0.90 and controller0.96. Shaft power leaves the thermal system.
These are demonstration maps, not selected motors, measured loss maps or thrust.

Distribution efficiency0.96; MPPT0.97. Their losses enter separate avionics
converter nodes. PV cell efficiency0.24 at25 C with assumed -0.0035/K coefficient
and0.15–0.28 clamps. Actual used electrical export is subtracted from the PV heat
balance. Curtailed/unharvested absorbed energy remains heat; it is not counted as
both exported power and PV heating. Battery thermal loss equals electrical to
chemical conversion loss, with assumed temperature-dependent efficiencies.
This is not an electrochemical Li-S model; reversible/entropic heat, cell spread,
aging, voltage/current dynamics and manufacturer maps are absent.

DC dispatch serves load from PV first; surplus charges up to12 kW with a taper
near92% SOC. A study-only charge permission ramps from0 at core0 C to1 at5 C.
Replace these illustrative thresholds with selected-cell data; they are NOT
Li-S allowable limits. Battery discharge supplies the deficit. SOC is not clipped
to hide an energy failure. Audits require0<SOC<1 over the entire run. Component
power maps remain active after temperature excursions: extreme-temperature
cases are conditional diagnostics, not predictions of functioning hardware.

6. HEATING / COOLING
Passive conduction and the two radiators remove waste heat. Ideal averaged
modulating heaters use P=enabled*clamp[gain*(T_set-T_sensor),0,Pmax]. There is no
hardware lag, discrete PWM, hysteresis, sensor error or redundant protection yet.
Avionics tray:3 C,100 W cap,40 W/K gain. Battery case:8 C,400 W,80 W/K.
Payload plate:0 C,120 W,40 W/K. Motor housing:-5 C,100 W,30 W/K.
These setpoints are control-study targets, not operational/survival limits.
A saturated heater can miss its target. Fixed conductive radiator paths continue
to cool at night. No coolant loop, heat pump or selected thermal-switch hardware.

7. CONTROLLED CASES
Reference; heading+90 deg; radiator direct exposure0.05->1; air-10 K; all heaters
off; payload electrical profilex2; avionics/payload strap conductancex0.35.
Each case changes one stated factor. Other system outputs respond through actual
thermal/electrical coupling. The cold case deliberately holds IR fixed and the
heading case holds aerodynamic power fixed. They are sensitivities, not complete
mission envelopes or flight predictions. These seven cases are not exhaustive
uncertainty quantification, material optimization or qualification.

8. NUMERICAL EVIDENCE AND LIMITS
Native Intel OpenModelica1.27.1 with Modelica4.1.0, DASSL, 60 s output/max step,
tolerance1e-7, eight days. Reference refinement uses30 s output/max step and1e-8.
Native event records are retained. Website final-day samples use a60 s grid and
post-event duplicate values. All extrema in the comparison table use raw final-
cycle records; interpolation can miss an intermediate native peak. Scientific
plots/CSV are exported from those native results; no browser solver is used.

Thermal audit: storage=sum C*(T-T0); sum external surface terms and generated
heat independently from the CSV. Thermal balance subtracts exported panel power.
Whole-system balance includes chemical storage and subtracts RF and shaft output.
Electrical dispatch closes instantaneously. Independent CSV quadrature is
compared with reconstructed storage; a separate audit reports native integral
residuals. Numerical closure and repeat-cycle convergence do not establish
physical accuracy. No parameter was fitted to experimental data.

Actual component limits, property uncertainties, spatial hot spots and physical
test measurements remain unavailable. No hardware pass/fail, environmental
qualification, readiness, company collaboration or endorsement is claimed.
